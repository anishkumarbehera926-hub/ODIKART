WHY ODICART?

More than food. It's heritage.

Odisha has a rich culinary heritage filled with dishes that are often prepared at home during festivals, celebrations and special occasions.

ODICart aims to introduce these traditional flavours to people beyond Odisha and help more people discover the stories behind them.
